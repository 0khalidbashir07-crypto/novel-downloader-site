Works beyond one site

Add any novel site yourself

If a site isn't handled out of the box, teach the app once: point it at the chapter list and the chapter text, check the live preview, and save. Every future link from that site just works.

  • Live preview before you save, so a wrong selector shows up immediately
  • Paste-the-page-source fallback when a site can't be fetched directly
  • Optional AI auto-detect: bring your own free Gemini key and it proposes the selectors for you
  • Remembers your settings between sessions
Runs on your computer

Private by design

It's a small local web app that opens in your browser. Downloads, saved sites, and your API key stay on your machine. Nothing is sent to a server we run.

  • Mac app you double-click to launch
  • Windows build available
  • No sign-up, no tracking
  • Optional password-protected hosting if you want it on your own server
